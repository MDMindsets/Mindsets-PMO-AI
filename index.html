What AI actually does in a PMO

Early warning, not post-mortems

Schedule and cost variance, milestone completion and risk entries are monitored continuously. A flag is raised when an initiative starts trending toward a RAG change — and when a delay in one initiative creates downstream risk in another.

Narrative insights at every refresh

Each cycle produces a written summary: what moved, why it moved, and what needs a decision. Cross-initiative patterns and systemic risks surfaced, not just numbers.

Reporting that runs itself

Board and department packs generated from live data on a schedule, using standardised templates, with version history and distribution logged.

Governed, with a human in the loop

Explainable outputs, an audit trail on every recommendation, and owner sign-off before anything reaches leadership.
